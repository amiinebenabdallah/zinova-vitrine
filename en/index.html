How Zinova is set up

Nothing to install on your computers: each person opens Zinova in a browser or installs it on a phone.

  1. We open your company's space

    At your own address, for example alpha.zinova.tn, with the modules you chose.

  2. Your starting data is loaded

    The reference list of pharmacies, parapharmacies and doctors is already there. We add your products, targets and teams.

  3. Everyone activates their account

    An email, a single-use link, a password chosen by the person. Management adds a 6-digit code.

  4. The field works, you see

    From the first day, check-ins, visits and reports arrive in your dashboard.

Your data stays inside your company.

Your pharmacy records, your sales and your bonuses are sensitive data. Zinova's security is built into the database, not added afterwards.

One company, one separate space

Double protection, in the application and in the database: a mistake gives an empty page, never another company's data.

Everyone sees their own work

Permissions follow the role. A promoter sees neither the sales figures nor the bonuses of her colleagues.

Two-factor authentication

Management confirms every sign-in with a 6-digit code. Everyone chooses their own password; nobody else knows it.

Photos deleted after the decision

The check-in photo disappears as soon as the manager approves or rejects it. The decision itself stays on record.

Every sensitive action recorded

A separate audit log that nobody can edit or erase.

No trackers

No advertising and no external analytics. French or English, according to each person's profile.
